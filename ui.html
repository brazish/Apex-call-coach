🎯 Opening — Build Urgency & Rapport
"I want you to know — reaching out today took courage, and I'm really glad you did. Can you tell me a little about what's been going on? I want to make sure we find the absolute best solution for your loved one."
🎯 Establish Authority Early
"We've helped hundreds of families in exactly your situation. What I've learned is that the families who act quickly are the ones who get their loved one back. So let's figure out together what's standing in the way."
🎯 Uncover the Pain
"How long has this been going on? Because I find that families usually wait 2-3 years longer than they should — and every day costs them more than any program ever would."
🚨 Objection: "It's too expensive"
"I completely understand — and I want to ask you something. What has this addiction already cost your family? Between the bail, the ER visits, the lost jobs, the broken relationships — most families tell me they've already spent more than the cost of treatment. This isn't an expense. It's an investment in getting your family back."
🚨 Objection: "I can't afford it right now"
"What does 'right now' mean to you — because addiction doesn't wait. Let me ask: if cost wasn't a factor, would you want your loved one to get help today? Good — then let's focus on making it work. We have financing options and our team works with families every day to find a way. What's your biggest concern about the investment?"
🚨 Objection: "That's more than I expected"
"I hear you — and I want to be transparent. You could find cheaper options, but what you get here is a clinical team that specializes in exactly what your loved one is going through, 24/7 support, and a track record of real results. Can I ask — what's your loved one's life worth to you?"
🚨 Objection: "I need to think about it"
"I respect that — and I also want to be honest with you as someone who does this every day. The families that say they need to think about it usually call back 3 months later after something bad happened. What specifically do you need to think through? Let's work through it together right now."
🚨 Objection: "I need to talk to my spouse"
"Absolutely — this is a family decision. Can we get them on the phone right now? I'd love to answer their questions directly and make sure you're both on the same page. It only takes 10 minutes and it will make your conversation much easier."
🚨 Objection: "Let me do more research"
"What are you hoping to find that would make you feel more confident? Because I'd rather answer those questions right now than have you spend hours on Google getting overwhelmed. What's the one thing that would make this a clear yes for you?"
🚨 Objection: "Will insurance cover this?"
"Great question — and the answer is it depends on your plan. What I can do is have our insurance specialist do a free verification right now while we're on the phone. It takes about 10 minutes and you'll know exactly what you're working with. Can I put you on a brief hold to get them?"
🚨 Objection: "Insurance denied it"
"That happens — and we fight it. We have a team that handles insurance appeals every single day and we win most of them. Don't let an insurance company make this decision for your family. Let's get you enrolled and we'll handle the insurance battle on your behalf."
🚨 Objection: "He's not ready"
"You know what — most people who come through our doors weren't ready either. Readiness comes after admission, not before. What we specialize in is meeting people exactly where they are. The question isn't whether he's ready — it's whether YOU are ready to give him the best chance at life."
🚨 Objection: "She needs to want it herself"
"I hear that all the time — and here's what 20 years of research tells us: waiting for someone to hit rock bottom costs lives. Intervention works. Structure works. Love works. You reaching out today IS her wanting help — she just doesn't know it yet. Let's get her in."
🚨 Objection: "We tried treatment before"
"That actually tells me a lot — it means your loved one has shown they can engage with treatment. Most people need multiple attempts before recovery sticks — that's not failure, that's the nature of this disease. What was missing last time? Because we can build a plan specifically designed to address that."
🎯 Assumptive Close
"Based on everything you've told me, it sounds like [program] is exactly what your family needs right now. Let's go ahead and get the admissions process started — I can have someone call you within the hour to handle the paperwork. Does that work?"
🎯 Urgency Close
"I want to be transparent — we have limited beds available and this time of year fills up fast. I'd hate for you to call back next week and not have a spot for your loved one. Can we reserve a spot today while you finalize the details?"
🎯 Emotional Close
"You called today because part of you knows this is the moment. You've been carrying this long enough. Let us carry it with you. What would it mean to your family to have your loved one back — really back — by the holidays?"
🎯 Final Objection Crusher
"Let me ask you one final question — if your loved one doesn't get help today, what happens next week? Next month? You already know the answer. The only question is whether today is the day you change that story. I believe it is. Let's do this together."
